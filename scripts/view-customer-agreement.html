Customer Agreement

The Customer Agreement service domain captures the legally binding terms accepted by a customer at onboarding and at material life events: master terms, regulatory disclosures, product-specific addenda, and consent records. Section boundaries on this page are reconstructed from the source document's table of contents because the export carries styled paragraphs rather than native headings.

Overview

Customer Agreement

What Is This Service Domain?

In BIAN 14.0, the Customer Agreement Service Domain maintains the master legal and regulatory agreements governing the overall relationship between the bank and a specific customer.

This agreement represents the overarching contractual framework under which one or more Sales Product Agreements are subsequently established. It acts as the primary legal contract and policy container between the bank and a customer.

The Customer Agreement:

  • Captures master legal terms, regulatory terms, policies and conditions
  • Defines regulatory, compliance, and jurisdictional considerations
  • Establishes governing provisions applicable across all product relationships
  • Serves as the contractual reference for all linked Sales Product Agreements

When to Use This Service Domain

Invoke this SD whenever a master contractual relationship must be established, validated, or maintained.

2.1 During Customer Onboarding (Pre-Product Setup)

Scenario: A new corporate customer is being onboarded. Before any account, loan, or facility is created, the master agreement must be executed.

Action: The orchestration layer invokes the Customer Agreement SD to initiate a Customer Agreement Control Record. This ensures:

  • Legal terms are established
  • Regulatory compliance clauses are accepted
  • Jurisdictional rules are recorded
  • Authorised signatories are captured

Only after the Customer Agreement CR is established can Sales Product Agreements be initiated.

2.2 During Product Origination

Scenario: A customer applies for a Personal Loan. Before initiating the Sales Product Agreement, the bank must confirm a valid Customer Agreement exists.

Action: The Sales Product Agreement SD invokes the Customer Agreement SD to:

  • Retrieve the active Customer Agreement instance
  • Validate that lending products are permitted under the master agreement
  • Confirm that the agreement status is not suspended or terminated
  • Verify that required regulatory clauses are included

2.3 During Compliance / Regulatory Review

Scenario: A regulatory rule change requires updated AML or cross-border provisions.

Action: The Customer Agreement SD is invoked to Update the master agreement terms. All linked Sales Product Agreements remain legally subordinate to this updated master agreement.

2.4 During Dispute or Legal Determination

Scenario: A dispute arises regarding liability terms.

Action: Operations retrieve the Customer Agreement CR to determine governing law, limitation of liability clauses, and arbitration mechanisms.

CR & BQ Structure

This is a Legal Agreement Management domain. For each master agreement established for a customer, a separate Customer Agreement Control Record (CR) will be created. For each structured component of the agreement, a separate Behaviour Qualifier (BQ) instance is created under the CR.

ComponentPurpose
Customer Agreement (CR)Master legal relationship record per customer. Captures agreement type, jurisdiction, validity period, signatories, and linked product agreements.
LegalTerms BQ (not implemented yet)General banking terms & conditions, liability clauses, dispute resolution, governing law. Supports 1:M, multiple law references per BQ.
RegulatoryTerms BQ (not implemented yet)KYC declaration, FATCA/CRS self-certification, data privacy consent, AML clauses. Supports 1:M, one CR to many BQ instances.
PolicyTerms BQ (not implemented yet)Bank policies on digital banking usage, fee revisions, service eligibility conditions. Supports 1:M, one CR to many BQ instances.

Example structure:

  • Customer Agreement (CR), Retail Banking Master Agreement
  • LegalTerms BQ, General T&Cs, liability, dispute resolution, governing law
  • RegulatoryTerms BQ, KYC, FATCA/CRS, data privacy consent
  • PolicyTerms BQ, Digital banking policy, fee revision policy, service eligibility

5.1 Lifecycle Update Triggers

The following events require update operations on the Customer Agreement:

  • Agreement terms are revised
  • Regulatory frameworks change (e.g. new AML rules, cross-border provisions)
  • Customer status changes impact agreement applicability
  • Legal reviews require structured updates
  • Authorised signatories change

Level of Details to Capture

This is a Legal Agreement Management domain, not a financial processing domain. Data captured must represent structured agreement metadata, not operational or transactional data.

BIAN Specification Enhancements & Proposed Changes

The following enhancements were applied while preserving BIAN structure:

EnhancementDetail
PartyReference → string (for v13, not needed for v14)PartyReference property modified to contain a string to avoid recursive loop generation in schema
AgreementSignatoriesResponsibleParties → arrayMade an array (1:M) to support multiple signatories per agreement
SalesProductAgreementReference → arrayMade an array (1:M) to support multiple product agreements linked to a single Customer Agreement CR
LegalTerms.ApplicableLawReference → arrayMade an array (1:M) to capture multiple legal agreements within one BQ
RegulatoryTerms BQ → 1:MEntire RegulatoryTerms BQ object supports 1:M relationship (one CR to many BQ instances). ApplicableRegulationReference also made an array.
PolicyTerms BQ → 1:MEntire PolicyTerms BQ object supports 1:M relationship (one CR to many BQ instances)
AgreementValidFromToDate → DateTimePeriodChanged data type from DateTime to DateTimePeriod so both agreement start and end date can be stored in a single field
Custom CIF Retrieve endpointAdded /CustomerAgreement/customerinformationprofile/{customerinformationprofileid}/Retrieve to allow retrieval using CIF ID instead of UUID
CIF ID stored in DBCIF ID is passed and stored in Customer Agreement table instead of UUID for direct lookup
Own DB schemaThis SD has its own DB schema to store data in accordance with BIAN API payloads
