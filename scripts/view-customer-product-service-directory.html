Customer Product & Service Directory

The Customer Product &amp; Service Directory service domain maintains the registry of products and services held by each customer. It is the holdings view across the customer relationship: which accounts, which agreements, which active services, with status.

What Is This Service Domain?

In BIAN v14.0, the Customer Product and Service Directory (CPSD) Service Domain maintains a consolidated directory, a catalog, of the most important details for the current products and services held by a specific customer.

Role: It acts as the master index or registry of customer holdings. It is the primary source for a customer 360 view of what products and services a customer owns.

Key Distinction

CPSD SD ManagesCPSD SD Does NOT Manage
Consolidated directory (registry) of customer holdingsOperational account balances, managed by Product SDs
Product instance references (account IDs) per customerContract terms and agreements, managed by Sales Product Agreement SD
Product metadata (type, name, status, version, features)Customer profile master data, managed by Party Directory SD
Service holdings per customer (AdministrativeService, FinancialService)Deep operational transaction data, managed by respective Product SDs
Lightweight eligibility reference for origination checks

Key Rule: CPSD stores lightweight reference data only. It does not duplicate operational data managed by Product SDs or contract data managed by Sales Product Agreement SD.

When to Use This Service Domain

Call the CPSD SD in any scenario requiring visibility of what products and services a customer holds, without querying the full Core Banking System.

ScenarioActionData Source / Target
Customer applies for gold account requiring an existing MortgageCustomer Offer SD queries CPSD to verify Mortgage product exists for Customer IDCPSD Product BQ, Retrieve
Customer logs into mobile banking dashboardApp calls CPSD to get list of active product instance IDs for displayCPSD CR + Product BQ, Retrieve
Relationship Manager reviews customer wallet shareQuery CPSD to see full spread of products the customer ownsCPSD CR + Product BQ, Retrieve
New account provisioned in CBSOrchestration layer registers Product BQ and publishes contract detailsCPSD Product BQ, Register + Update

During Origination (Eligibility Checks)

Scenario: A customer applies for a Gold status account which requires them to already hold a Mortgage.

Action: The Customer Offer SD queries CPSD to verify if a Mortgage product exists for that Customer ID. No need to query CBS directly.

During Servicing (Dashboard View)

Scenario: A customer logs into mobile banking. The app needs to display all their accounts (Loans, Cards, Savings) on the home screen.

Action: The app calls CPSD to get the list of active product instance IDs and associated metadata for the dashboard. A lightweight, fast query.

During Relationship Management (Wallet Share)

Scenario: A Relationship Manager needs to see the full spread of products a customer owns.

Action: They query CPSD to view the complete customer wallet share, including all product types and service holdings.

Level of Detail to Capture

CPSD is a Directory/Reference domain, not a processing domain. Data captured must be lightweight, not deep operational data.

LevelRecord TypeExample
Product TypeOne CR record per product typeSavings Account Product, Current Account Product
Account InstanceOne Product BQ record per accountSavings Basic Act 1, Savings Basic Act 2, Savings Premium Act 1
Service TypeOne CR record per service typeAdministrativeService, FinancialService
Service InstanceOne Service BQ record per serviceMonthly paper statement, Sweep service

Control Record (CR) per Customer

One CR record is created per customer, serving as the root directory entry. It captures:

  • CustomerReference (CIF), party identification linkage.
  • Party involvement role and validity period.
  • Directory entry status and validity.
  • ProductAgreementReference for agreement linkage.

Product BQ, One Per Account Instance

A separate Product BQ record is created for each account instance a customer holds. Key fields:

  • ProductInstanceReference, account number and issuing authority.
  • ProductIdentification, TM Product ID, open/close dates.
  • ProductName, ProductType, ProductDescription, ProductVersion.
  • ProductLifecycleStatus, current status with validity period (from/to).
  • ProductFeature, feature type, specification, name, and lifecycle status.

Service BQ, One Per Service Instance

A separate Service BQ record is created for each service the customer has opted for. The structure mirrors the Product BQ pattern. Service BQ is planned for a future implementation phase.

When and How to Invoke

CPSD must be invoked once core confirms customer creation (new Customer ID received from CBS) and again whenever an account is provisioned or updated.

StepEndpointPurpose
A, Register CRPOST .../RegisterCreate directory entry; assert customer creation. Call immediately on new Customer ID from CBS.
B, Register Product BQPOST .../Product/RegisterLink customer to Account ID. Skippable if customer created without account.
C, Update Product BQPUT .../Product/{id}/UpdatePublish contract/parameter specifics from CBS; enables downstream display without querying CBS.

Subsequent Lifecycle Update Triggers

The Product BQ Update endpoint must be called again on the following CBS events:

Trigger Event in CBSCPSD Update Required
Fee parameter change (e.g. inactivity_fee 5.00 → 6.00)PUT .../Product/{productid}/Update, update ProductFeature with new values
Account enters DormancyPUT .../Product/{productid}/Update, update ProductLifecycleStatus.StatusReason
Account marked pending closurePUT .../Product/{productid}/Update, update status and ValidityPeriod ToDateTime
Customer created without accountPOST .../Register (CR only), Product BQ registered later on account creation

Implementation notes on BIAN 14.0

The following changes were applied to BIAN v14.0 to support practical implementation:

ChangeDescription & Rationale
PartyReference → string (10 CR paths)Prevents recursive loop generation across multiple paths including EntryStatus, UsageLog, UpdateLog, EntryInstanceReference, CustomerReference, ServicerReference, and ServiceAgreementReference paths.
Product BQ → array (1:M)Enables 1 CR to hold many Product BQ records; real customers hold multiple accounts of different types.
Service BQ → array (1:M)Enables 1 CR to hold many Service BQ records; real customers may opt for multiple services.
Generic search by CustomerReferenceNon-standard BIAN endpoint to return all CR and BQ details for a CustomerReference; required for Customer 360 view.
Search by ProductInstanceReferenceNon-standard BIAN endpoint to return BQ Product details for a specific ProductInstanceReference (account number).

PartyReference paths converted to string:

  • CustomerReference.PartyReference
  • ServicerReference.PartyReference

Data Ownership & Persistence

The CPSD SD maintains its own database schema to store data in accordance with BIAN API payloads. This SD owns its data, it does not rely on CBS or other SDs for its stored data:

Schema EntityData Stored
Directory CR RecordsCustomerProductAndServiceDirectoryEntry, status, validity period, CustomerReference (CIF), ProductAgreementReference
Product BQ RecordsOne record per account instance: ProductInstanceReference, ProductIdentification, ProductName, ProductType, ProductLifecycleStatus, ProductVersion, ProductFeature
Service BQ RecordsOne record per service instance (same structure as Product BQ): service reference, type, lifecycle status
Status & Validity RecordsValidFromDate, ValidToDate, StatusReason for both CR and BQ records

Related Service Domains & Systems

Related SD / SystemRelationship
Product SDs (Current Account, Savings Account, Loan, etc.)Source of operational product data (balances, transactions). CPSD stores lightweight reference only; does not duplicate operational data.
Sales Product Agreement SDHolds formal contract terms. CPSD references the agreement via ProductAgreementReference but does not manage agreement content.
Customer Offer SDQueries CPSD during origination for eligibility checks (e.g. verify Mortgage product exists before offering Gold account).
Party Directory SDAuthoritative source of customer profile master data. CPSD references customer via CIF (CustomerReference.PartyReference).
Mobile Banking / Sales SystemsPrimary consumers of CPSD; query it for the Customer 360 dashboard view and wallet share without hitting CBS directly.
