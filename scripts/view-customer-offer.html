Customer Offer

The Customer Offer service domain captures the application context for a prospective product purchase: which product the customer is applying for, the application identifier, qualification context, and the offer outcome.

What Is This Service Domain?

In BIAN v14.0, the Customer Offer Service Domain manages the process of preparing and presenting a product or service offer to a customer. It acts as the offer orchestration layer that prepares the commercial proposal before a formal agreement is created.

The SD also holds details of assessments and correspondence shared with the customer, underwriting process decisions, credit rating and collateral submitted by the customer, and final product creation details upon successful offer acceptance.

Key Distinction

Customer Offer SD ManagesCustomer Offer SD Does NOT Manage
Offer preparation and configurationFinal Customer Agreement, Customer Agreement SD
Offer features and benefit termsSales Product Agreement, Sales Product Agreement SD
Offer workflow state and workstepsOperational product lifecycle, Product SDs
Underwriting and credit assessment referencesMaster customer profile, Party Directory SD
Involved party linkage (CIF reference)
Agreement and product initialisation linkage (BQ)

Illustrative Example

Customer: John Doe | Product Offered: Premium Savings Account | Offer includes: Interest rate proposal, Fee waiver for first year, Free debit card feature

When to Use This Service Domain

Call Customer Offer SD whenever a customer offer procedure must be initiated, configured, progressed, or linked to downstream agreements and products.

Trigger ScenarioActionSD Invoked
Customer applies for Savings Account digitallyGenerate offer procedure; capture product and features optedCustomer Offer SD, Initiate
Relationship manager proposes product upgradeGenerate upgrade offer with revised rates and featuresCustomer Offer SD, Update
Customer accepts offerUpdate workflow status; trigger agreement creationCustomer Offer SD, Update
Agreement createdLink agreement IDs to offer recordCustomer Offer SD, Agreement BQ
Product account provisionedRecord account number against offerCustomer Offer SD, ProductInitialization BQ

During Product Origination

Scenario: A customer applies for a Savings Account through a digital onboarding journey.

Action: The orchestration layer calls Customer Offer SD to generate the offer procedure, capturing the product selected and features opted.

During Product Upgrade / Relationship Management

Scenario: A relationship manager proposes upgrading a customer from Standard Savings Account to Premium Savings Account.

Action: Customer Offer SD generates a special upgrade offer containing revised interest rates and additional features.

Control Record Structure

A CustomerOfferProcedure Control Record represents the entire lifecycle of a single customer offer. Key components:

CR ComponentDescription
CustomerOfferProcedureRoot Control Record representing the entire lifecycle of one customer offer
CustomerOfferReferenceConverted to string; carries Application ID; full offer object sits in CustomerOfferInvolvement
CustomerOfferProcessingTaskTracks workstep type, status, description, datetime, and work product for each lifecycle stage
CustomerOfferInvolvedPartyCaptures party reference (name, type, CIF), offer details, amount, and OfferTermAndCondition[]
ProductandServiceReferenceRecords product type, ID, name, description, and version associated with the offer
CustomerReferenceCaptures customer relationship lifecycle status and validity period

Offer Feature & Workflow Details

Offer Feature Types (OfferTermAndCondition[])

An offer may contain multiple configurable features captured as an array. Example feature types:

Feature TypeFeature NameDescription
FeeFeatureFreeDebitCardComplimentary debit card for first year
InterestFeaturePromotionalInterestIncreased promotional interest rate
ServiceFeatureFreeStatementFree monthly account statement

Offer Processing Worksteps (CustomerOfferProcessingTask)

The SD tracks processing steps through the offer lifecycle. Supported workstep types:

Workstep TypeDescription
EligibilityCheckVerify product eligibility for the customer
FeatureSelectionConfigure product features for the offer
OfferPresentationPresent the configured offer to the customer
OfferAcceptanceCustomer formally accepts the offer

Offer Lifecycle Flow

Customer Offer SD is invoked during the product sales journey, before agreement creation:

Customer Onboarding / Sales Journey → Customer Offer Creation → Customer Accepts Offer → Customer & Sales Product Agreement Creation

Step A, Initiate the Offer (CR)

Action: POST /CustomerOffer/Initiate

Purpose: Create a CustomerOfferProcedure instance to represent the proposed offer.

  • Generates Application ID in CustomerOfferReference.CustomerOffer.
  • Sets initial WorkstepStatus to InProgress.
  • Records CustomerReference lifecycle status and offer start date.

Step B, Update Offer Configuration (CR)

Action: PUT /CustomerOffer/{customerofferid}/Update

Purpose: Modify offer configuration. Examples:

◦ Feature Configuration, add FreeDebitCard feature (FeatureAmount: 1000 INR).

◦ Pricing Update, change promotional offer rate (OfferRate: 4%, Percentage).

◦ Workflow Update, advance WorkstepType: OfferPresentation, Status: Completed.

◦ Party Capture, record CIF-linked involved party from PRDD.

◦ Product Reference, log product type, identifier, version, and description.

BQ Agreement, Link Agreements

Action: PUT /CustomerOffer/{customerofferid}/Agreement/{agreementid}/Update

Purpose: Update the offer with created Customer Agreement ID and Sales Product Agreement ID.

BQ ProductInitialization, Record Account Number

Action: PUT /CustomerOffer/{customerofferid}/ProductInitialization/{productinitializationid}/Update

Purpose: Update the offer with the provisioned account number once the product has been created downstream.

Level of Detail to Capture

Customer Offer SD is a processing domain, not a master data or operational domain. Data captured must focus on offer configuration and workflow state, not operational product data.

  • Offer-specific, all data must relate to a specific offer instance (CustomerOfferProcedure).
  • Workflow-accurate, workstep status and dates must reflect current offer processing state.
  • Feature-complete, all offered features and terms captured in OfferTermAndCondition[].
  • Party-linked, involved party must be traceable to CIF in PRDD via PartyIdentification.
  • Agreement-linked, once agreements are created, their IDs must be recorded in the BQ.
