Payment Order Initiation

The Payment Order Initiation service domain creates, tracks, and dispatches outbound payment instructions. It captures the originator, beneficiary, amount, value date, and rail selection, and emits the payment to the appropriate scheme adapter.

What Is This Service Domain?

In BIAN v14.0, the Payment Order Initiation Service Domain manages the capture, registration, and initiation of customer payment orders across all banking channels.

Role: It acts as the primary entry-point Processing Domain that receives a payment instruction from a channel or orchestration layer, enriches it with system-controlled attributes, and creates a traceable instance record of that payment order.

Classification: This is a Transaction / Processing Domain, not a Catalog or Directory Domain. It processes and registers payment instructions; it does not manage product definitions, account state, or party profiles.

Key Distinction

Payment Order Initiation SD ManagesPayment Order Initiation SD Does NOT Manage
Capture and registration of payment instructionsFund clearing or settlement
Creation of Payment Order Instance RecordsAccount balance management (Savings / Current Account SDs)
Unique Payment Order Reference generationParty or account master data management
Payment instruction enrichment with system-controlled attributesPayment routing logic (Payments Engine domain)
Traceability via Instance Reference and Transaction IDTransaction history or ledger records (Position Keeping SD)
Payment status reporting (Initiated) at registrationBehavior Qualifier sub-records, none at Initiation step

Payment Order Types

A payment order processed by this SD may represent:

Payment Order TypeDescription
Customer-to-customer fund transferRetail / Consumer transfer between two customer accounts
Bill paymentCustomer payment towards a bill or obligation
ACH Credit TransferStandard batch-settled credit transfer via ACH network
Teller-initiated paymentBank operator processes payment on behalf of walk-in customer
Internet / Mobile Banking paymentCustomer-initiated transfer via digital channel

When to Use This Service Domain

Invoke this SD whenever a channel or system submits a new payment instruction for registration and downstream processing:

Trigger EventWhen to Call POST /PaymentOrderInitiation/Initiate
Customer initiates transfer via mobile / internet bankingChannel collects payer, payee, amount, currency, datetime and submits to Initiate endpoint
Bank teller initiates payment on behalf of customerBank system submits customer payment details; channel enriches payload and calls Initiate
Bank funds customer account from internal accountInternal account used as payer; customer account used as payee; Initiate called by orchestration layer
Self transfer between customer's own accountsCustomer's two own accounts used as payer and payee; same Initiate endpoint used

Use Case A, Customer-Initiated Fund Transfer (Mobile Banking)

Scenario: A retail customer initiates a transfer via the mobile banking app.

Action: The mobile app collects payer, payee, amount, currency, and payment datetime. The channel enriches the payload with pre-configured fields (DateTimeType = CreatingDate; PaymentMechanism = Book Transfer) and calls POST /PaymentOrderInitiation/Initiate.

Response: Unique Payment Instance Reference (e.g., POI-2026-00041823) and Payment Instance Status = Initiated.

Use Case B, Bank Operator-Initiated Payment

Scenario: A bank operator processes a payment instruction on behalf of a walk-in customer.

Action: The bank system submits the customer's payment details. The channel enriches the payload with pre-configured fields and calls the BIAN Initiate endpoint. The payment is registered and a unique Instance Reference is returned.

Level of Detail to Capture

Data captured must meet the following quality requirements:

Data Quality RequirementExplanation
Payment instruction-specificAll fields relate to a single, specific payment instruction event
Instruction-levelNot account-level or party-level data, captures the transfer instruction only
Traceable by unique Instance ReferenceEvery payment order receives a unique Payment Order Reference (e.g., POI-2026-00041823)
Minimal at initiationOnly the fields required to register and post the instruction are captured; no BQ sub-records at this stage

Control Record Structure

All payment order fields are captured within the CR payload. There are no BQ sub-records at the Initiation step. The CR groups fields into the following logical areas:

CR Field GroupFields Captured
Payer ReferencePayerReference.PayerIdentifier (name), PayerBankReference.PayerBankIdentifier (bank name)
Payer AccountFinancialTransactionFromAccount, AccountIdentification (account number, account type enum)
Payee ReferencePayeeReference.PayeeIdentifier (name), PayeeBankReference.PayeeBankIdentifier (bank name)
Payee AccountFinancialTransactionTargetAccount, AccountIdentification (account number, account type enum)
Payment TransactionAmount (AmountValue), Currency (Currencycode), DateTime (DateTimeContent, DateTimeType = CreatingDate), Mechanism (Book Transfer)
Payment PurposePaymentPurpose, customer-provided remittance information / remarks (alphanumeric)
Payment Identification (Response)PaymentTransactionIdentification[0] = TransactionID (CBS UUID); PaymentTransactionIdentification[1] = PaymentOrderID (adapter-generated)
Payment Status (Response)PaymentTransactionStatus.StatusReason = Initiated (demo flow only)

Response ID rule: PaymentTransactionIdentification[0] = TransactionID (CBS UUID); PaymentTransactionIdentification[1] = PaymentOrderID (adapter-generated, same as Client Batch ID submitted to CBS).

Payment Workflow, Current Scope

Use Case 2, Self Transfer (Customer's Own Accounts)

The customer transfers funds between two accounts they own. The payer account and payee account both belong to the same customer. The adapter generates a unique Payment Order ID, submits a Book Transfer posting instruction to CBS, and returns the Payment Order Reference and Transaction ID.

Use Case 3, Funding from Bank's Internal Account

The bank funds a customer account from an internal bank account. The payer is the bank's internal account; the payee is the customer account. The same Initiate endpoint and field mapping apply.

Use Case 1, Intrabank Transfer (Out of Scope)

Transfer between two different customers at the same bank. This use case is planned for future implementation and is not part of the current demo scope.

Core Banking System (CBS / Thought Machine)Receives the posting instruction from the adapter. Returns Posting Instruction Batch UUID (Transaction ID). CBS performs the actual account posting.
Payments Engine (Future)Not integrated in current demo scope. Will be the downstream routing engine for ACH, RTP, FedNow, SWIFT, and SEPA mechanisms in future phases.
